### Verified correct, with limits

- **Blitz run/pass gate:** the base sampler selects first; the wrapper redraws only rows with `play_type == "pass"`, from pass-only pools. It preserves the selected row’s run/pass class. Extra RNG consumption means identical seeds need not produce identical subsequent trajectories. “CALLED pass” is slightly broader language than the implemented classification: `qb_scramble` does not participate in that gate.
- **Fourth down:** observed field-position × distance cells, field-position-only and team-wide fallbacks, and hard-sparse fallback are implemented. The decision masks FGs beyond `yardline_100 + 18 > 66`. Sparse fallback text says `default_punt` even when returning `go`; behavior is explicit but the text is inaccurate.
- **FGs:** make/miss sampling uses current distance `yardline_100 + 18`, then ±5, ±10, and flagged team-wide fallback. This is distance-conditioned empirical resampling, not a fitted kick-distance curve. The missed-FG helper implements the requested LOS+7 spot and receiving-own-20 floor.
- **Defense and leakage:** weight `0.5` is explicitly labeled `STRUCTURAL_ASSUMPTION`; default configs disable opponent-allowed mixing. `build_asof_history_for_game` filters **both offense and defense rows to `week < target week`**, same season. This establishes play-week exclusion, not historical availability/versioning of later-pulled charting.
- **Punt microfix:** NaN flags are handled without truthiness conversion; a sampled touchback places the receiver at `yardline_100=80`. Existing regressions pass.
- **Basic player credit:** sacks receive no passing-yard credit; negative completions retain negative passing/receiving yards; rushing gains are credited before a lost-fumble exit. The two P1 issues prevent treating overall player outputs as valid.
- **Catalog construction/delegation:** all identified production simulation/grading callers use `build_catalog`. `__getattr__` exposes missing fourth-down and defense attributes. Clock and defense methods explicitly synchronize their shadowed wrapper attributes; I found no additional reproducible delegation failure in those paths.
- **Grading:** as-of PPG baseline arithmetic, reported W1 skips, development labeling, and home-*non-win* tie handling are consistent. The MC SE `sample SD / sqrt(n)` is valid **conditional on this fixed simulator and catalog**, for that score mean. It excludes data, model, and structural uncertainty. The finite-simulation Brier estimate also contains small Monte Carlo estimation error.
- **Config independence:** data-only does not invoke QB adjustments; script-adjusted does so before catalog construction. Market fields are not wired into sampling, and the market config discloses that. It therefore supplies no market-conditioned comparison or independent market evidence.
