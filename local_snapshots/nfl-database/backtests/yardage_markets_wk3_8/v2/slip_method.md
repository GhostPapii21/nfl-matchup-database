# Frozen slip method (both variants)

Apply locked market-side rules, optional pre-registered edgecap, and optional labeled Sharp HINDSIGHT filter. Missing price/edge abstains. Select one leg per normalized player per week: highest directional model edge, then highest American price, then ascending candidate ID. Sort by week ascending, edge descending, price descending, ID ascending. Form weekly slips by scanning the remaining ordered pool, taking up to four legs with at most two per game. Emit a three-leg slip if only three eligible legs remain; fewer than three stay unused. Remove emitted legs; do not reuse players across slips in a week. Singles and slips are separate portfolios.

$10 per single/slip. Decimal odds = 1 + American/100 for positive prices, or 1 + 100/abs(American) for negative prices. Multiply decimal odds for slip payout; profit = $10 × (product - 1). A losing leg loses the stake. Push legs contribute odds 1; all pushes refund. Any ungraded leg leaves the slip pending (zero accounted stake). Hit% excludes pushes/pending; ROI = profit / settled stake. Book SGP repricing, participation voids, and correlation are unavailable; payouts are illustrative. Sharp ON uses Oct27 man rates only as HINDSIGHT, never as point-in-time evidence; missing rates abstain.
