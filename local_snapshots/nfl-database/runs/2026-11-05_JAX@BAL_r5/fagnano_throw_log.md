## What he was doing (pbp descriptions + public recaps)
- **Came in cold, 2Q ~9:40, after Huntley's toe injury.** His first two throws worked: Cuevas 15 and Lane 13, both under 1-man pressure. Then the pocket collapsed: 3 sacks in 2Q (L.Jackson, T.Parker, Rousseau), two of them on 5-man rushes and one on a 4-man rush.
- **Everything went short.** Hill's targets were behind the line or at it (aDOT -3.3; screens and checkdowns). NBC calls his catches "back-to-back receptions during a two-minute drill... second and third and longs." Andrews got short in-breakers (aDOT 3.5). His only "deep"-labelled balls went to Bateman: one 18-air catch, one 16-air incompletion.
- **Flowers: 0 catches on 4 targets, plus the INT** (15 air, 2Q 1:21). This was Flowers' first career 0-catch game (baltimoreravens.com).
- **Henry: 1 checkdown for 6 yards.** He's not a receiving factor.
