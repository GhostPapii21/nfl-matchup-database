          What Are Advanced Cornerback Stats?

          Advanced cornerback stats provide a deeper analysis of a cornerback’s performance beyond traditional NFL player stats like tackles and interceptions. For NFL player stats, these include metrics such as Passer Rating Allowed, which measures the quarterback’s efficiency when targeting that cornerback, and Completion Percentage Allowed, showing how often passes thrown their way are caught. Another key stat is Coverage Snap Percentage, indicating how often a cornerback is targeted in coverage. For NFL team stats, advanced cornerback metrics help evaluate a team’s overall pass defense strength and strategy. These advanced stats give coaches and analysts clearer insights into a cornerback’s true impact on the game.
