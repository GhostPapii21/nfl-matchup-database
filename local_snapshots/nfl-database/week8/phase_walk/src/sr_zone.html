            What is Zone Coverage Rate?

            Zone coverage rate is the percentage of a defense’s coverage snaps played in zone coverage. It highlights whether a team leans on pattern-matching and landmark-based drops rather than man-to-man assignments.

            Related: This Week's WR/CB Matchups

            Which teams have the highest zone coverage rate in the NFL in 2026?

            Seattle leads the NFL in zone coverage rate at 82.3%, through games played October 30, 2026. Washington is second at 81.7%, Pittsburgh third at 80.2%, Minnesota fourth at 78.9% & Arizona fifth at 74.3%. Seattle's rate is 0.6 percentage points above second place & 24.2 percentage points above the 25th-ranked team.

            Only 14.7 percentage points separate first from tenth. Buffalo is sixth at 73.7%, Tennessee seventh at 71.8%, Jacksonville eighth at 70.9%, New England ninth at 67.9% & Miami tenth at 67.6%.

            Arizona ranks fifth on the season at 74.3% but has led the league in zone coverage rate over the last three weeks at 82.0%. Baltimore is 11th at 67.3%, Tampa Bay 12th at 66.9%, San Francisco 13th at 66.5%, Las Vegas 14th at 65.7%, the Los Angeles Chargers 15th at 65.4%, Philadelphia 16th at 65.4%, New Orleans 17th at 65.1% & Green Bay 18th at 64.2%.

            Eight teams sit inside 2.5 percentage points of each other from ninth to 16th, the tightest stretch of the ranking. Atlanta is 19th at 63.8%, the New York Jets 20th at 62.5%, Houston 21st at 61.9%, Detroit 22nd at 61.7%, Chicago 23rd at 60.2%, Carolina 24th at 59.7% & the Los Angeles Rams 25th at 58.1%.
