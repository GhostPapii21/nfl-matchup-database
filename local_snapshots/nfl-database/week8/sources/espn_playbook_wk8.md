The Playbook is your one-stop shop for all your fantasy football decisions for Week 8, which kicks off Thursday night with a game between the [Panthers and Packers](https://www.espn.com/nfl/game/_/gameId/401873023/panthers-packers).

This column features score projections, over/unders, win probabilities, and of course, easily digestible fantasy advice for seasonlong leagues and DFS. This guide should help you with all sorts of decision-making, including sit/start advice, last-minute waiver adds and lineup choices.

Additionally, we have folded our Shadow Reports into the game-by-game breakdowns here. Using our play-by-play data, we're able to identify defensive schemes and where each wide receiver and cornerback lines up on each play. By tracking these WR/CB matchups, including potential shadow situations, we can offer the best fantasy guidance each week.

* * *

- _If you want a more detailed look at some of this data, be sure to check out the weekly [**Shadow Reports: WR vs. CB Cheat Sheet**](https://g.espncdn.com/s/ffldraftkit/26/NFL26_CS_WRCB.pdf?adddata=2026CS_WRCB)_. _You can also take a look at the analysis of each team's **[strength of schedule](https://www.espn.com/fantasy/football/story/_/id/50117108/fantasy-football-2026-strength-schedule-rest-season)** for the remainder of the season._


* * *

All of this advice is centered on 12-team PPR leagues with relatively standard scoring and lineup settings (1 QB, 2 RB, 3 WR, 1 Flex, 1 K, 1 D/ST), although I'll often mention "shallow" or "deep" leagues for some starters. The charts show all players who have been projected for at least 6.0 fantasy points this week, as well as all D/STs. "Matchup" is automatically determined using a proprietary metric that factors in raw and volume-adjusted fantasy points allowed to each position by the opposing defense.

* * *

- _**DFS values in the charts below (and their relative quality) are provided only for Sunday's main slate, which is why there are no values for games scheduled on days other than Sunday after 1 p.m. ET.**_

## ![](https://a.espncdn.com/combiner/i?img=/i/teamlogos/nfl/500/CAR.png&w=110&h=110)![](https://a.espncdn.com/combiner/i?img=/i/teamlogos/nfl/500/GB.png&w=110&h=110)**[Carolina Panthers](https://www.espn.com/nfl/team/_/name/CAR/Carolina-Panthers) @ [Green Bay Packers](https://www.espn.com/nfl/team/_/name/GB/Green-Bay-Packers)**

**Projected score:** Packers 25, Panthers 22

**Lineup locks: [Chuba Hubbard](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/4241416/chuba-hubbard), [Josh Jacobs](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/4047365/josh-jacobs), [Tetairoa McMillan](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/4685472/tetairoa-mcmillan), [Matthew Golden](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/4701936/matthew-golden), [Christian Watson](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/4248528/christian-watson), [Jalen Coker](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/4695883/jalen-coker), [Tucker Kraft](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/4572680/tucker-kraft)**

**Fantasy Scoop:** Jacobs made his 2026 debut on Sunday and was busy, playing on 35 snaps (54%) and totaling 11 carries and seven targets. The veteran back didn't do much on the ground (36 yards) but had the most productive receiving game of his career (5-55-1 on seven targets). It's hard to imagine Jacobs will be a consistent factor in the passing game as he totaled just 36 catches during both of his first two seasons in Green Bay and now has three career receiving TDs. Still, his rushing workload will only expand and he's always going to be a factor near the goal line (31 TDs in 33 games with the Packers).

Jacobs can be valued as a fringe RB1 in an excellent Week 8 matchup against a Carolina defense that sits top three in yards, TDs (nine) and fantasy points allowed to RBs. Backs are averaging a league-high 5.2 yards per carry against the Panthers.

**Over/under:** 46.7 (9th highest)

**Win probability:** Packers 58% (9th highest)

## ![](https://a.espncdn.com/combiner/i?img=/i/teamlogos/nfl/500/BAL.png&w=110&h=110)![](https://a.espncdn.com/combiner/i?img=/i/teamlogos/nfl/500/BUF.png&w=110&h=110)**[Baltimore Ravens](https://www.espn.com/nfl/team/_/name/BAL/Baltimore-Ravens) @ [Buffalo Bills](https://www.espn.com/nfl/team/_/name/BUF/Buffalo-Bills)**

**Projected score:** Bills 33, Ravens 30

**Lineup locks: [Josh Allen](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/3918298/josh-allen), [Lamar Jackson](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/3916387/lamar-jackson), [Derrick Henry](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/3043078/derrick-henry), [James Cook III](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/4379399/james-cook), [Zay Flowers](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/4429615/zay-flowers), [DJ Moore](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/3915416/dj-moore)**

**Fantasy Scoop:** Henry remains a lineup lock despite falling short of 6.0 fantasy points in consecutive games for the first time since Weeks 10-11 of the 2023 season (while he was still with the Titans). Henry's very limited receiving role leads to the occasional dud -- he's been held under 12.0 fantasy points nine times in 24 games since the start of last season -- but that's more than offset by huge games thanks to his elite rushing and scoring ability (19-plus points in nine of those 24 outings).

Henry opened the 2026 season with 15.9-plus fantasy points in five consecutive games and, though his career-low 3.9 yards per carry is mildly concerning, he's a candidate for a leap forward with Jackson expected back under center. He's in an especially good spot this week against a Buffalo defense that sits dead last in EPA and that has allowed nine TDs (second most) and the third-most fantasy points to running backs. Buffalo has surrendered at least one RB score in all six games.

**Over/under:** 63.1 (Highest)

**Win probability:** Bills 58% (10th highest)

## ![](https://a.espncdn.com/combiner/i?img=/i/teamlogos/nfl/500/TEN.png&w=110&h=110)![](https://a.espncdn.com/combiner/i?img=/i/teamlogos/nfl/500/CIN.png&w=110&h=110)**[Tennessee Titans](https://www.espn.com/nfl/team/_/name/TEN/Tennessee-Titans) @ [Cincinnati Bengals](https://www.espn.com/nfl/team/_/name/CIN/Cincinnati-Bengals)**

**Projected score:** Bengals 29, Titans 14

**Lineup locks: [Joe Burrow](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/3915511/joe-burrow), [Chase Brown](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/4362238/chase-brown), [Ja'Marr Chase](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/4362628/jamarr-chase), [Tee Higgins](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/4239993/tee-higgins)**

**Fantasy Scoop:** Tate is a strong bet for shadow coverage courtesy of [DJ Turner II](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/4572036/dj-turner-ii) this week. On Sunday, [Zay Flowers](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/4429615/zay-flowers) was able to produce the first game of over 15 fantasy points by a wide receiver against Cincinnati this season, but note that Flowers did nearly all of that damage while away from Turner's coverage -- he had one catch for 6 yards on 10 matchups with Turner.

Flowers (54% slot) benefits from a versatile role, whereas Tate (28% slot) will see Turner on nearly three-quarters of his routes. The 14 receivers Turner shadowed in 2025 averaged just 8.8 fantasy PPG and he's also limited [Emeka Egbuka](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/4567750/emeka-egbuka) (11.3 points), [DK Metcalf](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/4047650/dk-metcalf) (12.1) and [Parker Washington](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/4432620/parker-washington) (2.0) already in 2026. Tate should be downgraded in what will be one of his toughest matchups of the season.

**Over/under:** 42.5 (11th highest)

**Win probability:** Bengals 94% (Highest)

## ![](https://a.espncdn.com/combiner/i?img=/i/teamlogos/nfl/500/ARI.png&w=110&h=110)![](https://a.espncdn.com/combiner/i?img=/i/teamlogos/nfl/500/DAL.png&w=110&h=110)**[Arizona Cardinals](https://www.espn.com/nfl/team/_/name/ARI/Arizona-Cardinals) @ [Dallas Cowboys](https://www.espn.com/nfl/team/_/name/DAL/Dallas-Cowboys)**

**Projected score:** Cowboys 33, Cardinals 21

**Lineup locks: [Javonte Williams](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/4361579/javonte-williams), [CeeDee Lamb](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/4241389/ceedee-lamb), [George Pickens](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/4426354/george-pickens), [Michael Wilson](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/4360761/michael-wilson), [Trey McBride](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/4361307/trey-mcbride)**

**Fantasy Scoop:** [Dak Prescott](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/2577417/dak-prescott)'s output has been a mixed bag thus far. He's delivered just two top-10 fantasy outings: a 29.8 effort against Washington in Week 2 and 23.3 points at Philadelphia on Monday night. Those two strong outings bookended a four-game stretch in which Prescott had as many interceptions (four) as he did passing TDs (exactly one in each of the four games). Prescott added some value with his legs early on (59 yards on 10 carries during his first three games), but that has dipped over the past four weeks (22 yards on six carries).

The good news is that Prescott is coming off a good game, leading a very fast-moving and pass-first offense, and is set up with a terrific Week 8 matchup against an Arizona defense that has allowed 19 total TDs and the second-most QB fantasy points. Prescott is a strong streaming option.

**Over/under:** 54.3 (2nd highest)

**Win probability:** Cowboys 86% (3rd highest)

## ![](https://a.espncdn.com/combiner/i?img=/i/teamlogos/nfl/500/MIN.png&w=110&h=110)![](https://a.espncdn.com/combiner/i?img=/i/teamlogos/nfl/500/DET.png&w=110&h=110)**[Minnesota Vikings](https://www.espn.com/nfl/team/_/name/MIN/Minnesota-Vikings) @ [Detroit Lions](https://www.espn.com/nfl/team/_/name/DET/Detroit-Lions)**

**Projected score:** Lions 26, Vikings 24

**Lineup locks: [Jahmyr Gibbs](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/4429795/jahmyr-gibbs), [Justin Jefferson](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/4262921/justin-jefferson),** **[Amon-Ra St. Brown](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/4374302/amon-ra-st-brown)** **, [Sam LaPorta](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/4430027/sam-laporta)**

**Fantasy Scoop:** [T.J. Hockenson](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/4036133/tj-hockenson) caught four out of five targets for 35 yards and one touchdown against the Colts on Sunday. It was yet another strong performance for the veteran tight end after he posted a 13-119-0 receiving line on 13 targets in his last full game with [Kyler Murray](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/3917315/kyler-murray) under center (Miami, Week 4), though it's worth noting that Jefferson was sidelined for that game. Of course, even if we exclude that Week 4 game, Hockenson is handling a solid 20% target share, which has allowed a pair of 13.5-plus-point efforts.

Just like when he was in Arizona, Murray hasn't been shy about his attraction to throwing to tight ends, so Hockenson is very much back in the TE1 mix. That's especially the case this week against a Detroit defense that has allowed the most targets, catches, yardage, TDs (10!) and fantasy points to tight ends.

**Over/under:** 49.6 (4th highest)

**Win probability:** Lions 56% (13th highest)

## ![](https://a.espncdn.com/combiner/i?img=/i/teamlogos/nfl/500/LV.png&w=110&h=110)![](https://a.espncdn.com/combiner/i?img=/i/teamlogos/nfl/500/NYJ.png&w=110&h=110)**[Las Vegas Raiders](https://www.espn.com/nfl/team/_/name/LV/Las-Vegas-Raiders) @ [New York Jets](https://www.espn.com/nfl/team/_/name/NYJ/New-York-Jets)**

**Projected score:** Jets 28, Raiders 26

**Lineup locks: [Ashton Jeanty](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/4890973/ashton-jeanty), [Breece Hall](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/4427366/breece-hall), [Garrett Wilson](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/4569618/garrett-wilson), [Brock Bowers](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/4432665/brock-bowers)**

**Fantasy Scoop:** [Geno Smith](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/15864/geno-smith) is fresh off a strong fantasy performance in which he completed 23 of his 30 passing attempts for 287 yards and three touchdowns. Smith took advantage of a shaky Miami pass defense, and, not coincidentally, his only other good fantasy outing of the season came in an even better matchup (26.0 points against Detroit in Week 3). Aside from those two games, however, Smith is averaging an ugly 11.1 fantasy points per game, with four of those five outings coming against defenses ranked 21st or lower in QB fantasy PPG.

Although it's clear that Smith isn't close to being an every-week fantasy option, the good news is that he has another very good matchup this week. The Raiders have allowed the sixth-most passing TDs (16) and fifth-most QB fantasy points this season, with [Justin Herbert](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/4038941/justin-herbert) (Week 2) the only QB who failed to reach 16.5 points against them. Smith's lack of rushing (22-42-0 this season) limits his output, but he's a terrific Week 8 streamer in a "revenge game" against Las Vegas.

**Over/under:** 53.2 (3rd highest)

**Win probability:** Jets 57% (12th highest)

## ![](https://a.espncdn.com/combiner/i?img=/i/teamlogos/nfl/500/CLE.png&w=110&h=110)![](https://a.espncdn.com/combiner/i?img=/i/teamlogos/nfl/500/PIT.png&w=110&h=110)**[Cleveland Browns](https://www.espn.com/nfl/team/_/name/CLE/Cleveland-Browns) @ [Pittsburgh Steelers](https://www.espn.com/nfl/team/_/name/PIT/Pittsburgh-Steelers)**

**Projected score:** Steelers 23, Browns 16

**Lineup locks: [Jaylen Warren](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/4569987/jaylen-warren), [DK Metcalf](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/4047650/dk-metcalf), [Denzel Boston](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/4832800/denzel-boston)**

**Fantasy Scoop:** [Dylan Sampson](https://www.espn.com/nfl/player/_/id/5081397/dylan-sampson) was busy in his return from injury on Sunday.
