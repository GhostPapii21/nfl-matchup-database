We're less than two weeks away from the [2026 NFL trade deadline](https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/50043471/2026-trade-deadline-updates-latest-news-deals-terms) (Tuesday, Nov. 10, at 4 p.m. ET). Which players could potentially be available? And which trade candidates could make the biggest impact with new teams if they do get moved?

NFL analyst Matt Bowen and national NFL reporter Jeremy Fowler worked together to build a comprehensive list of the top 25 possible trade candidates. To be clear: Players are ranked here by the _potential value_ they'd provide a new team if traded -- not their likelihood of actually being dealt.
