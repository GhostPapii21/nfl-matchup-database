# Brainstorm context from CBS coordinator rankings (added Nov 3, 2026)

Context for the brainstorm step only. These aren't standalone rules.

Source: CBS Sports, Garrett Podell, 'Ranking NFL's best new coordinators' (Nov 3, 2026): https://www.cbssports.com/nfl/news/nfl-coordinator-rankings-midseason-packers-jonathan-gannon-steelers/

## BAL
- Offense scores 15.8 points a game without Lamar Jackson (since Week 5), 5th-lowest; 29.0 a game in Weeks 1-4 with him. Lost 3 straight. (OC Declan Doyle)

## NYG
- Defense: blitz rate 41.9% (3rd-highest), 6.1 yards per attempt allowed (2nd-lowest). Checkdown profile: lean RB/TE receptions, caution on deep WR yards. Brian Burns out for the season since Week 3. (DC Dennard Wilson)
- Offense: Nabers has 4 straight 100+ yard games through a QB carousel (Dart, Winston, McCarthy). (OC Matt Nagy)

## PIT
- Defense: run defense 3rd (83.9 rushing yards allowed a game), league-high 26 sacks, 18.0 points a game allowed (3rd). Held CLE to 34 rushing yards in Week 8. T.J. Watt has 7.5 sacks. (DC Patrick Graham)

## SF
- Defense: run defense 5th (86.8 a game), total defense 4th (299.3), 19.9 points a game allowed (6th). (DC Raheem Morris)

## CLE
- Defense: 38.8% pressure rate (10th) while blitzing only 28.2% (12th-lowest); 21 sacks; 48% red-zone TD rate allowed. QB-vs-blitz splits undersell this pass rush, so use pressure splits for opponents. (DC Mike Rutenberg)

## GB
- Offense: worst rushing offense in the league (60.6 a game). Defense on the field 31:36 a game (3rd-most). Defense allows 6.2 yards per attempt (4th) and 78.2 passer rating (4th), with 15 takeaways (2nd). Nixon allows a 69.6 passer rating as primary defender (7th of 52). Parsons (ACL) not back until around Thanksgiving; Edgerrin Cooper (Achilles) out for the season. (DC Jonathan Gannon)

## SEA
- Darnold: -0.67 EPA per dropback under pressure, worst in the NFL. JSN: 900+ yards and 9+ TDs through 8 games. Charbonnet (ACL) and Price (chest) first active together in Week 8. (OC Brian Fleury)

## KC
- Offense runs through Kenneth Walker (770 rushing yards, 3rd; 5.5 YPC, 2nd; 59 tackles avoided). Kelce (37) is the top target. (OC Eric Bieniemy)

## DET
- Gibbs has three 150+ yard rushing games in 2026 under OC Drew Petzing; no Montgomery. Offense scores 29.0 a game (3rd).
