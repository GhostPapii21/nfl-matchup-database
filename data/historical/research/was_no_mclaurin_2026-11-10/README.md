## 3. Did Diggs' catches and yards go up, and who else benefited?

**Diggs' catches and yards did not rise in a clear way. The sample is small.**

In the three games McLaurin missed and Diggs played (Weeks 4, 8, 9) he went **5-35, 3-38 and 3-51**: 3.7 catches and 41.3 yards per game, on a 20.6% target share. In his three games *with* McLaurin (Weeks 1, 2, 3) he went **4-55, 5-47 and 4-33**: 4.3 catches and 45.0 yards, on a 23.7% share. In the two Daniels-started games without McLaurin he had **exactly 3 catches both times** (38 and 51 yards), both under the 4.5 consensus receptions line. His biggest catch game of the year was Week 6 (6-44), when McLaurin was already down to 7 snaps. Target share was roughly stable (see `target_share.png`).

**Who else produced more without McLaurin:**

- **Antonio Williams — usage clearly up; catches only sometimes.** Offensive snap share went from 37 / 49 / 41% in Weeks 1–3 to **69% (W4), 83% (W8) and 84% (W9)**. Targets per game went from 3.5 with McLaurin to **7.0 without**. Receptions moved less: 3.0 per game with McLaurin vs 3.7 without (3, 5 and 3 catches in Weeks 4, 8 and 9). He has one 4+ catch game in each bucket (4 in W1, 5 in W8).
- **Van Jefferson** is the late add. He had 3 snaps in Week 5 and 7 in Week 8, then **7 targets, 5-58-1 in Week 9** (36 snaps, 58%) after Burks and Brown sat. That is one game.
- **Treylon Burks** had two explosive-yardage games in the no-McLaurin / limited window: 1-47 (Week 4, Kaliakmanis) and **3-81 (Week 8, Daniels)**. He was **out Week 9**, so he has not been a locked-in weekly replacement.
- **Chig Okonkwo** was already producing with McLaurin in Weeks 5–6 (4-60 and 7-69). Without McLaurin he has been uneven: 2-5 (W4), 2-32 (W8), 6-56 (W9). This is not a simple "he only eats when McLaurin sits" pattern.
- **Dyami Brown's** one big game (3-65 on 4 targets) was Week 4 with Kaliakmanis, not Daniels, and he was out Week 9.
