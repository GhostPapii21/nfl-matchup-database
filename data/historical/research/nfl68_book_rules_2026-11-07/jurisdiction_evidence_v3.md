## Settlement rules found (NC, NY)
- **FanDuel NC** (https://www.fanduel.com/fanduel-sportsbook-house-rules-nc, eff. Oct 15 2026) and **FanDuel NY** (https://www.fanduel.com/fanduel-sportsbook-house-rules-ny, eff. Oct 15 2026):
  - Player not playing: "only when a player does not play a snap in that game are the selections voided." Kicking-points props are an exception.
  - Whole number: only the game-level Spread/Total refund rule. A push on an integer player-yardage line is an **inference**, not a quoted rule.
  - Parlays/SGP: void legs are removed; if all legs but one are void, the bet settles as a straight. No player-prop push rule for parlays.
- **DraftKings NC/NY:** UNCONFIRMED. No state rule page retrieved. The DK push text found earlier comes from a Virginia-scoped page and is not portable.
- **BetMGM NC/NY:** UNCONFIRMED. The 2023 MA PDF (whole SGP void, QB must start, gross passing yards) is neither current nor state-portable.
- **Zero/negative yards:** no rule found for any book.
