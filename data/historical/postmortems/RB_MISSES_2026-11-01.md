## 3) Root causes (systematic-debugging, evidence above)
1. **No "every lead back gets priced" invariant (NYJ).** The LV@NYJ run selected legs from the pass game only. Nothing in PHASE_SCRIPT Core step 4 (Opportunity Gate) or run_gate.py checks that each team's top projected-carries RB has a leg evaluated. Allen surfaced only by accident in the bonus sweep.
2. **Injury promotion doesn't trigger a re-projection (NYJ, TEN).** Core step 0 (injury & status) and Phase 15 inactives only *log* outs (Hall, Spears). The volume for the next man up is still L3 share with the injured player active, so carry projections run low. Allen's sims (~72) were built on a split role. As the bell-cow he got 20 carries.
3. **Single-model median veto plus a conservative Bayes prior (PIT, NYJ).** The Phase 14 rule "a model median does not clear" let the Bayesian model (63.0 vs MC/GR ~72.5) kill Warren alone. The Bayes prior shrinks RB rushing toward league average and ignored CLE's run D. Same pattern on Pollard (67.5 vs ~77).
4. **Underdog/trailing-script blocks are applied blanket (TEN).** The Phase 6/7/13 10G rules cut Pollard even though all three medians cleared. A 10-pt dog whose backup RB is out keeps nearly all the RB carries even when trailing, and Tennessee isn't trailing.
5. **Split flag is risk-only and never re-priced (PIT).** The Dowdle trend lowered our confidence but never fed a two-back carry model. Script (PIT -3.5 in a low total, leading) favored both backs. Today's new game-script RB rule (7+ fav + 4.4 YPC or weather flag) wouldn't have fired here: PIT was -3.5 and NYJ -1.5.
6. **Opponent run-D YPC is weighted only as a suppressor (≤3.8, Core step 6), never as a boost.** Soft run Ds don't raise projected YPC.

## 4) Proposed fixes (proposals only, need Rico/Ghost6 approval)
- **A. Injury-promotion rule (Phase 0/15):** when an RB1 is OUT/IR, auto-build RB2: (a) carries = team RB carries × (own share + 70–80% of the injured back's share), (b) rush, rush+rec and alt rungs priced in all three sims, (c) re-run at each inactives check. Regression fixtures: Allen Wk8 (Hall out) and Pollard Wk8 (Spears out).
- **B. Lead-back coverage check in run_gate.py:** fail the run if any team's projected carries leader has no evaluated rush leg (rush yds, rush+rec, and the lowest rung within -250). Test: LV@NYJ today has to FAIL.
- **C. Median veto → consensus:** for RB rushing, cut at Phase 14 only if 2 of 3 medians miss the line, or if the averaged median misses. Flag a Bayes outlier >8 yds from MC/GR as MODEL-SUSPECT to debug. Fixture: Warren O64.5 must pass to pricing.
- **D. Dog-RB exception (Phase 6/7/13):** a rush over on an underdog is allowed when all 3 medians clear AND (backup RB out OR RB share ≥70%), on a modest rung (e.g. Pollard 49.5/59.5).
- **E. Split re-price:** when a split flag fires (RB2 snaps up 2 straight weeks), model both backs with a carry-share distribution instead of only flagging. Price the lead back on lower alts.
- **F. Run-D boost:** mirror the ≤3.8 suppressor with a ≥4.4 YPC-allowed boost to projected YPC, independent of spread.
- **Tie-in with the new game-script RB rule:** keep that rule as is (7+ fav AND opp 4.4+ YPC, or weather flag → boost carries, model RB2, Phase 7 can't solo-cut). A and F cover the cases it misses: small spreads (PIT -3.5, NYJ -1.5) and injury-driven volume. B guarantees the rule has a priced leg to act on.
