2. **`mix_equal_0.25` promotion is not independent of inspected W7–8.**

   The dev calculation genuinely selects the mixture under the recorded minimax-KL criterion: worst-cell KL **0.275526**, versus **0.281193** for the next candidate. I found no arithmetic contradiction there.

   However, [validate.py](/workspace/wt-nfl-sim-core-2026-11-06/research/possession_engine_v0/runs/dal_ind_suite_fix_2026-11-06/validate.py:45) explicitly requires the selected candidate’s **W7–8 IND carry KL** to satisfy a threshold before accepting it; otherwise, the script falls back to equal weights. Thus W7–8 participates in the final policy decision. It is more than reporting an inspected holdout.

   A preregistered gate still uses its evaluation data for acceptance. Moreover, preregistration within a repair after W7–8 was already inspected cannot establish freedom from analyst adaptation in candidate choice, objective, threshold, or singled-out role.

   Historical evaluation also filters identities through the prospective availability roster before counting events. The train-only-universe audit improves the W6 evidence, but does not redo the original candidate selection under historically valid identity support.

   **Finding:** reproducible retrospective selection, with disclosed contamination; **not leak-safe prospective validation**. The self-check’s characterization of W7–8 as reporting-only is too strong.

3. **Team-score invariance has credible architectural support, but incomplete coverage.**

   Separating attribution RNG from team RNG addresses the identified input-hash reseeding confound. The real paired runs compare team-period statistics per trial, which is stronger than comparing means. The synthetic test also requires changed player credits with unchanged team paths.

   Nevertheless, the empirical evidence is **five common trial indices**, across three variants, plus identical repeats. Those repeats establish reproducibility; they add no independent scenario coverage.

   The synthetic test changes passer/target weights for a targeted-pass fixture. The real comparison removes half overrides consistently. Neither establishes coverage across carry changes, half overrides, all terminals, overtime, availability edge cases, or multiple role-state structures.

   **Finding:** supported invariance for the tested rotation-weight perturbations. “This establishes engineering invariance” needs a scoped qualifier. It does not establish predictive accuracy or invariance under every role-related model change.

4. **`UNSUPPORTED` is adequate containment for research, not completed semantics.**

   Rejecting nonzero penalty and return fields is a useful safeguard. Declaring official overtime unsupported is honest. Conservation tests then verify internal accounting under the implemented model.

   Those safeguards do not establish that the resulting score distribution adequately represents games containing omitted events. Absence or rejection of an explicit field does not resolve how historical effects enter fitted outcome distributions.

   Overtime remains especially consequential: the engine executes an acknowledged approximation while declaring official overtime unsupported. A limitation label documents that behavior; it does not validate it.

   **Finding:** acceptable restricted research acceptance with explicit limitations. **Insufficient for official-rules acceptance or unrestricted forecast certification.** Keeping `DEFERRED_100K` is justified; greater simulation volume would not repair these semantics.

5. **Remaining genuine blockers**

   - **Independent predictive evaluation:** freeze the complete policy, eligibility procedure, acceptance criteria, and implementation before evaluating genuinely unseen games.
   - **Historical eligibility validity:** extend the train-only support approach to selection and broader validation; report excluded and unseen-event mass.
   - **Compatibility and replay:** demonstrate consumer handling of legacy error envelopes, joint results, and seed-version migration. Frozen-source hash preservation alone does not prove old artifacts replay successfully.
   - **Broader invariance coverage:** exercise relevant terminal, half, role, and overtime paths with paired comparisons.
   - **Rules and distribution validity:** implement or justify omitted semantics and quantify predictive consequences.
   - **Activation remains outstanding:** staging inputs and documenting copy commands do not demonstrate external deployment.
