# SHARED_ACCOUNTING_V1

Opt-in API: simulate.simulate_games with joint_producer.shared_accounting=true, existing frozen joint spec/input manifest/root/output_root. The joint producer samples empirical usage/outcome tables and empirical role weights; shared_accounting.shared_projection consumes those exact events, calls joint_accounting.derive_tables and validate_trial, and rejects mismatched or invalid tables. It produces TEAM_STATS/PLAYER_STATS tables, event/period/registry record_id opportunity ledger, aggregates, seed and trial IDs. There is one forecast_id and one trial_id per draw. No separate empirical trial is sampled in this mode: equality is by common event authority, checked against derive_tables and all conservation invariants. Targets/carries/pass_attempts and pass/receiving/rush yards retain joint credit rules, including explicit unassigned residuals. Scores retain event period attribution, including OT and tries.

Legacy independent simulate_one yard-row mode remains available and separate; its old n=500 DAL@IND artifacts are not shared-mode forecasts and are not relabeled. No equality between independently sampled trials is claimed. Before/after audit is the same current engine with previous depth roles versus newly fitted roles. Both have zero conservation and bridge failures, not evidence of a historical bridge that previously existed.

Remaining gaps: no penalties/returns/lateral fitted support, official OT entitlement, fitted target depth or try model, coverage/routes/personnel or NGS integration. Missing source features keep allows_sim_effect=false. Shared accounting is implemented; complete football semantics and prospective inputs remain unfinished. DEFERRED_100K_UNFINISHED_SEMANTICS.
