## Limits

- Receiving legs: main lines in this data are unders only; overs exist only as alt rungs (many rungs per player, heavily correlated; the game-cluster bootstrap absorbs part of that).
- Rule 1 cannot filter the baseline (the baseline is rushing legs).
- Rule 2 here uses play-by-play only (by-role yards allowed, run YPC rank), walk-forward. Sharp coverage shares are season-to-date as of Nov 24 and would leak, so they are not backtested. Slot/outside alignment is unknown.
- Rule 3: no saved hourly rain-RATE forecasts (NWS or Open-Meteo) cover Weeks 4-9; the first proxy is the nflverse kickoff observation (precip words in the weather text, wind >= 15 mph). No rain rate, not hourly, not a forecast: the hourly grade itself is untested. `2026_MNF_CHI_SEA` has no play-by-play game row, so its weather is unknown.
- Rule 3 second proxy: box-local saved Open-Meteo historical-forecast kickoff-window pulls for Weeks 6-8 (42 games; max sustained wind, max gust, max precip probability). The rain amount was not saved, so this is wind/gust only; still not the hourly rain-rate grade.
- Weeks 8-9 were already looked at by earlier studies; W4-7 is the baseline's fit sample.

Conclusion rule: a rule may become a cut only when its filter beats the baseline with n>=30 and a paired diff CI lower bound > 0, and Rico approves. All three stay flags.
