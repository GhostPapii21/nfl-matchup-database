## What changes across scenarios

- Hockenson's audited 102 routes/151 Minnesota dropbacks = **67.5% route participation**; 23 targets/102 routes and historical Wentz target catch rate 29/37; 4.5-reception line remains close to the B forecast 4.4. BUF blitz could increase short routes or force TE pass protection.
- Jefferson's two Wentz 2026 appearances gave 12/39 targets; Addison's 2025–26 Wentz eligible games gave 37/188 targets. QB dropbacks and third-down sacks must be jointly assessed before projecting receivers.
- Wentz has 14 third-down sacks across 70 dropbacks in 2025–26. His receiving volume does not automatically collapse: Jefferson topped 70 yards in all five 2025 Wentz starts, but only three cleared 77.5. Historical not future hit probability.
- Minnesota opposing TEs had just 22 receptions/273 yards/0 TDs across seven games; Buffalo opponent TEs 43 receptions/494 yards/1 TD. Team-level role matchups only; no verified assigned CB/S shadow.
- Minnesota's high zone/blitz frequency can help Shakir hot routes or Allen scrambles, but edge contain and BUF questionable LT/guard could instead generate quick throws/sacks.
- Aaron Jones 25+ rush+receive: conditional scenarios 34/39/33 yards, more forgiving than his 43.5 mainstream line, but no live alt-price verified; Jones' pass-down routes compete with DeeJay Dallas.
- Bills Cook carries/yardage most favorable in script C, while higher Wentz throws lift MIN receptions.
