## Mechanism checks

**Jones and Mason:** Mason was activated Week7 after injured-reserve thumb surgery. Jones aggravated a knee in Week7. Week8 Jones played about 38% of offensive snaps with 5 carries/2 catches, Mason about 28% with eight carries, DeeJay Dallas 34% with 9 routes to Jones' seven. This is a three-way committee, not a two-back monopoly. No Week9 medical designation for Jones/Mason. Mason 47.5 under can fail if Jones' workload falls further.

**Addison / Hockenson:** Both returned Week7 (hamstring and concussion protocol). Addison's Week8 routes were 31/33 (94%); the primary warning is 3/8 catches and a 51-yard play supplying 77% of his 66 yards. Hockenson Week8 routes were 22/33 (67%); he had 4/58. No Week9 designation for either; the Hockenson 4.5 receptions line depends on Wentz's dropbacks and TE blocking role.

**Jefferson:** Missed Week4 with an ankle injury but ran all 33 routes Week8, seeing nine targets for 33 yards. The injury is not evidence of a current restriction. It demonstrates target volume without efficient yardage (3.7 yards per target) even before tonight's Wentz change.

**Moore:** Missed Weeks5–6 shoulder injury; Week8 return gave seven targets, four catches and 82 yards on 32 routes/38 BUF dropbacks (~84%), but late exit/evaluation. Shoulder was limited Thu/Fri/Sat Week9, no game designation. Week8 longest gain was 49 of 82 yards, indicating explosive-play concentration. A 25% hypothetical opportunity loss takes B=54 receiving yards to 40.5, under 47.5 market.

**Coleman:** Shoulder LP each Week9 practice but no game status; Week8 24 routes/38 BUF dropbacks (63%), 3 targets 1 catch 4 yards. B=30 vs27.5 yards is not a robust over.

**Kincaid:** Chest injury Week6–8; Week8 21/38 routes (55%) and 57% snaps despite 4/86 and a touchdown. Full practices Fri and Sat for Week9. Minnesota allowed 22 TE catches/273 yards/0 touchdowns across 7 games, position-wide. B=38 vs36.5 is an extremely narrow over case.

**Cook, Allen and Buffalo line:** Cook underwent a Week8 head evaluation, was cleared, and carries no Week9 designation. LT Dion Dawkins ribs Q (DNP all week), LG Alec Anderson ankle Q (LP Saturday), while RT Spencer Brown back returns FP, no designation. B Cook projection 87 yards on assumed 20 carries would be 81 with a hypothetical 0.3 drop in YPC (NOT a forecast injury modifier). Allen B 33 rushing vs30.5 also has little margin and is highly contain-sensitive.

**Shakir:** Absence Friday was *personal*, not injury, full Saturday. Week8 30/38 routes (~79%), 5 catches on six targets for 64 yards. Do not incorrectly give medical penalty.
