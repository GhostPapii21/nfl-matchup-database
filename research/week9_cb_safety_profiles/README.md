# NFL 2026 Week 9 defender research build

Status: **INTEGRATION_READY_FORMAT**. Not model-ready. As of November 7, 2026, for Sunday November 8. No models, picks, forecasts, or coverage assignments computed.

## Files and keys

- `DEFENDER_PROFILES.csv`: one normalized name/team row per relevant DB. Universe includes current public depth chart players, latest local roster DBs, W5–8 roster DBs, and season charted DBs. `profile_scope` distinguishes these; unlisted historical players are not asserted current. GSIS `player_id` is missing where identity is unverified. Current depth starters sort first within team, then backups by rank, then unranked players.
- `DEPTH_CHART_ROWS.csv`: every CB/S/FS/SS/DB/NB source slot, including duplicate player listings across positions. `depth_rank` is the order within that explicit slot. `role` is starter for rank 1, backup otherwise. NB ranks exist only where ESPN explicitly lists NB. Generic DB labels are marked ambiguous. Profile uses the best listed rank; this table preserves all slots.
- `DEFENDER_GAME_OBSERVATIONS.csv`: unique GSIS player_id × game_id, using team at that game, W5–8 only. Roster membership creates a row even when participation is unconfirmed; no bye-week game is invented. `roster_status` is the weekly roster designation, not current injury status. `snaps` = defensive snaps, joined from snap_counts by PFR player ID + team through an unambiguous GSIS roster mapping, with unique normalized name + team fallback where roster PFR ID is absent. `snaps_join_method` records which method resolved each row.
- `DEFENDER_RECEIVER_OBSERVATIONS.csv`: per defender/play/evidence_kind. A player can receive both PBU and INT evidence for one play. Tackle credits on a completion are deduplicated within evidence_kind. Receiver name/ID, receiving yards, yards gained, and description come directly from that play. Missing intended receiver on an INT/PBU remains NULL. Completion yards describe the play, never yards charged to that defender.
- `OPPONENT_RECEIVER_CONTEXT.csv`: separate WR target context for each historical opponent game. Target share = named WR targets / all named receiver team targets in eligible pbp, including targets to RB/TE in denominator. Sacks, throwaways with no receiver, no-plays, and special teams are excluded. Position is the exact week/opponent roster WR classification. No defender is assigned these targets.
- `MATCHUP_VERIFICATION_SEPARATE.csv`: published Week 9 PFF projected associations from the 135-row local file. These remain projections; `coverage_assignment_verified=false`. They contribute no observed metrics or depth ranks.
- `SOURCES_AND_GAPS.csv`: local inventory, sources actually read, every web query/fetch including Washington's 404 and corrected WSH success, exclusions, and per-team gaps. `raw/` contains only public depth-page HTML fetched with curl, without cookies/auth. HTML tables alone were parsed; scripts and embedded state were not used.
- `PFF_PROFILE_CANDIDATES_AUDIT.csv`, `PFR_EXCLUDED_2TM.csv`, `SNAP_ID_JOIN_GAPS.csv`, `TEAM_COUNTS.csv`, and `local_capture_search.txt`: supporting audits. `build.py` reproduces the tables deterministically from frozen inputs/captures; `fetch_depth.py` refreshes depth pages separately.

## Metric definitions, source groups, and windows

Empty CSV cells are NULL, never the literal word MISSING or a replacement zero. Each non-null metric has a source in its group or metric-specific `_source` field. `IDENTITY_ALIAS_AUDIT.csv` records abbreviated/formal-name bridges verified by matching ESPN public player links or cached PFR player IDs to roster IDs within the same team. Depth source names remain in `depth_player_as_shown` and `source_player`. PFR source names remain in `PFR_player`; their canonical-name/team join is unambiguous. Player identity uses exact normalized name plus normalized team after these ID-verified bridges; punctuation, accents and suffixes Jr/Sr/II/III/IV/V are removed, with no fuzzy alias guesses. Source-side duplicates are excluded. PFR team aliases TAM/NWE/GNB/KAN/LVR/NOR/SFO/LA/WSH normalize to manifest codes.

`PFR_SEASON_*` preserves source columns verbatim: G games credited, GS starts, Pass Coverage_Int interceptions, Tgt targets, Cmp completions, Cmp% completion percentage, Yds yards, Yds/Cmp and Yds/Tgt averages, TD touchdowns, Rat passer rating, DADOT average depth of target, Air air yards, YAC yards after catch; Pass Rush and Tackles columns retain provider definitions. `PFR_source`, `PFR_window`, `PFR_as_of` apply to every PFR metric. Only unambiguous name + team joins are imported; 2TM excluded. This is season charted attribution, not game coverage. Cache mtime **2026-11-04 07:02 ET is user-supplied; exact season cutoff is unverified**. Filename date is not a cutoff. No PFR request was made.

`PFF_SEASON_rec_allowed`, `rating_allowed`, `ypr_allowed`, `pbu`, `cov_snaps`, `total_snaps` are public free profile fields only. The group's `_source`, `_url`, `_as_of`, `_window`, `_flags` apply to all populated PFF metrics. Selection priority: Nov 7 live-profile capture CSV, then individual visible-panel capture, then original free-profile markdown. No cross-window filling between candidates. A detailed visible 0 is preserved; a No Data panel is not converted to 0. Coverage snaps are not routes or targets; public rating denominator is generally not displayed. Provider PFR/PFF totals may disagree and remain separate. PFF flags preserve sample caveats. Profile date and season endpoints are not interchangeable.

The [official nflfastR pbp dictionary](https://nflfastr.com/reference/fast_scraper.html) distinguishes solo, assisted, and tackle-with-assist credits. Game `solo_tackles` counts distinct explicit solo_tackle_1/2 credits. `assisted_tackles` counts assist_tackle_1–4 credits. `tackles_with_assist` separately preserves tackle_with_assist_1/2 credits; these are not silently merged into the solo definition. This is an explicit pbp credit tally, not a reconstructed official box-score tackle total. `OBSERVED_PBU` counts pass_defense_1/2 credits; `OBSERVED_INTERCEPTION` counts interception_player_id credits; `forced_fumbles` counts forced_fumble_player_1/2 credits. Duplicate same metric/player/play credits count once. All counts use nondeleted regular-season defensive scrimmage plays, excluding no_play and special teams, with tackle/FF team credit required to equal defteam. INT and PBU use defteam. No lateral interception attribution is synthesized. Metric-specific source fields and `metric_window=game_id` cover these counts. Zero counts are emitted only for defenders with positive defensive snaps or an explicit defensive event in that game; rows lacking confirmed defensive participation retain NULL event counts. An explicitly recorded snap value 0 remains a measured 0. Event counts can still be limited by provider credit completeness; absence of credit is not film evidence.

Individual game `targets_allowed`, `receptions_allowed`, `yards_allowed`, `explosives_allowed` stay NULL because no eligible game-level charted source was found. `coverage_missing_reason` states this gap. Public pbp and the supplied FTN schema do not identify the coverage defender. No most-targeted DB, tackle, pregame primary matchup, side/alignment proxy, or team total is promoted to charted coverage. Local defender ledgers explicitly identify inferred associations and first-tackler proxies; excluded from coverage values. Local capture search preserves original paths, dates and source text for review. Week10 season/projection captures are not W5–8 game charts.

`TEAM_CONTEXT_pass_20plus_allowed` = number of completed passes with receiving_yards >=20 against the defense in that game, all receiver positions. Group source/window fields apply. It is repeated team context, never an individual allocation. `OBSERVED_TACKLE_AFTER_CATCH` proves a tackle credit on the same completed-pass play, not coverage responsibility. Receiver events carry `source` and `window`; their credit_metric records which explicit credit supplied the evidence.

Depth `depth_source`, `depth_as_of`, `depth_window` refer to the live Nov 7 public ESPN table. Publisher exact update timestamps were not displayed. `availability` is source injury tag: IR/out/questionable/doubtful/suspended or no_designation; no_designation does not establish health or Sunday active status. `role` remains the listed depth role even when a listed starter is out/IR. Local roster status/week/source remain distinct and may be older. No inactives for Sunday are assumed.
