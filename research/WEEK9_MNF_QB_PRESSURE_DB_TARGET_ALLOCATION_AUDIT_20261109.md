# Bills at Vikings · QB pressure → coverage → target allocation pregame diagnostic

**Pregame as-of:** Monday 2026-11-09 ~07:00 ET. **Kickoff:** 20:15 ET. No 2026 Week 9 postgame plays or stats. Research-only; **not a fitted main or Lightning projection**, certified live line, calibrated win probability, or verified shadow-CB assignment.

## The matchup must be modeled in two directions

- **Buffalo offense against Minnesota:** Vikings 73.6% blitz, 46.4% pressure, 25 sacks in seven games per TruMedia/Bills official preview; 86.5% zone, 5.4% man, 63.4% middle-open in archived Sharp team snapshot (Nov 3). Vikings secondary: CB Isaiah Rodgers, Byron Murphy Jr., James Pierre; safety Harrison Smith, Joshua Metellus, Theo Jackson and Jay Ward, last two questionable. Pressure disguises can force fast throws *or* leave an escape lane for Allen. This is not proof of a Shakir or Kincaid receiving over, nor of Allen 30.5 rushing over.
- **Minnesota offense against Buffalo:** Bills 254.7 net passing yards allowed/game (seven games). Their archived Sharp sample says 72.6% zone, 19.3% man and roughly half MOFO/MOFC; cannot infer tomorrow's shells from aggregate. Bills CB Christian Benford (outside), Davison Igbinosun/Maxwell Hairston (outside), Dee Alford (nickel), safeties Cole Bishop and Damar Hamlin with Jalon Kilgore rotated. Buffalo released safety C.J. Gardner-Johnson Nov 3. **No verified snap-by-snap shadow coverage/individual defender receiving-yards allowed:** do not assign Jefferson exclusively to Igbinosun or Addison exclusively to Benford; Minnesota moves receivers and Buffalo zones.
- **Sudden BUF scheme change:** Bills official Next Gen Stats reported 64.5% blitz on Baltimore Week 8, whereas Buffalo had not blitzed above 39% in any of Weeks 1–6. This is a two-scenario risk, not evidence Buffalo will necessarily repeat that blitz rate tonight. Last week's Ravens played multiple QBs; 68 net passing yards allowed is not clean proof of improved shutdown coverage.

## Receivers against actual defenders: roles, not manufactured shadows

**Jefferson:** Possible routes against outside CBs Benford, Igbinosun and Hairston, and nickel Alford when condensed/inside, with Bishop/Hamlin help. Christian Benford is not a verified exploitable weak CB. Some free matchup ranks disagree with film/coverage grades for Igbinosun; no validated individual 2026 week-by-week target/yards coverage exposure is staged. Team-level BUF passing allowance is favorable, but recently better DB play (13 pass breakups in Weeks 6 & 8). WR1 examples against BUF: Amon-Ra 9-142, Nacua 17-138/24 targets. High Jefferson **target ceiling**, yardage range wide. At 77.5 yards and 6.5 receptions, the game script and Wentz completion quality are the swing factors, not automatic overs.

**Addison:** Likely mainly outside CB group (Benford or Igbinosun/Hairston), not proven one-on-one. Historical Wentz share 19.7% and 2025 Wentz 21 catches/30 targets contradict a firm **under 3.5 receptions** based on 2026 2/7 alone. Strongest value is conditional: >=30 Wentz attempts puts four catches in play; if pressured into 20–24 attempts, receptions under gains appeal. His explosive yardage depends on protection, target depth, and split-safety rotations. A dated 39.5 yards line cannot be called strong either direction without route/air-yard and price.

**Hockenson:** Receives across LB Terrel Bernard/Dorian Williams and zone safety help (Bishop/Hamlin), plus nickel rotations; no guaranteed single defender. BUF opposing TEs 43 catches/494 yds/1 TD on 64 targets through seven; Rams and Raiders account for 275 yards, so group mean alone overstates typical individual TE exposure. Wentz targets Hockenson ~17.8% historically and caught 29/37 (78.4%). This **downgrades the previously strong under 4.5 receptions** to a CONDITIONAL PASS. A 38-pass script creates approximately 5+ expected catches from historical ratios, while 24 throws yields ~3.3. Under blitz, stay-in blocking is the principal counterargument. Route participation, chip/block assignment and pressure-aligned reads are not yet audited.

**Josh Allen vs Minnesota:** Bills official historical blitz split since 2024: ~63% completions, 8.3 yards/attempt, 26 TD, 9 INT, 103.0 rating. In 2026 specifically, independent November pregame analysis reported 65.7% completions, 8.4 YPA, 3 TD and 3 INT vs five-plus rushers, with 3 scrambles for 25 yards. Flores specifically warned that unchecked pressure can give Allen an escape route, though he plans contain discipline. This gives two opposing mechanisms for Allen 30.5 rush yards: free rushers + man backside can generate long scrambles, but quick passes and disciplined edges can suppress them. Blitz frequency is not scramble probability. Bills LT Dawkins and LG Anderson QUESTIONABLE, amplifying protection uncertainty. Cook rushes and Shakir quick routes also likely pressure counters, but need pregame role confirmation. MIN CB Rodgers and Murphy, safeties Smith/Metellus, backed by questionable Jackson/Ward, blitz/drop disguise means any specific WR-CB pairing is CONDITIONAL, never charted fact.
