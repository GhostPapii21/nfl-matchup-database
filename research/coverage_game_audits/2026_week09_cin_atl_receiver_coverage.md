## Provisional game read

Chase's logged results include a deep-right touchdown and short targets that generated substantial YAC, especially the 31-yard catch with 26 YAC. This documents where the Bengals gained yards, but does not establish whether Atlanta called man, zone, a split-safety shell, or a specific rotation on those plays.

Higgins converted three short targets in the live snapshot available to the prior audit, including a short touchdown. That supports a low-depth/low-YAC description for that snapshot; it does not by itself establish that Henderson or another defender suppressed his explosive opportunities.
