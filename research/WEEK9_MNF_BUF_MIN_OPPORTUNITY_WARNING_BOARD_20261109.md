## What actually matters

- **Minnesota defense:** 73.6% blitzes and 46.4% pressures on dropbacks (TruMedia via Bills official preview), 25 sacks in seven games; archived Sharp team sample 86.5% zone and 63.4% middle-open. These do not establish individual defender assignments or which passes will be underneath.
- **Minnesota opposition rushing:** 116.7 rush yards/game, 4.54 yds/carry. Detroit exploited it with 230 team rush yards on 39 carries (Gibbs 153 on 23). Yet Detroit scored only 19. A strong rushing day is not automatically a high TD or high team-scoring day.
- **Minnesota opposition tight ends:** 22 receptions, 273 yards, **zero receiving TDs** in seven; Green Bay accounted for 115 of the yards. Remaining six games averaged only 26.3 combined TE yards. That is a TE-group rate, NOT Dalton Kincaid's individual deterministic ceiling.
- **Buffalo defense:** 254.7 net pass yards/game allowed; 97.4 rush yards/game and 3.85 yds/carry. Opposing TEs: 43 catches, 494 yards, 1 TD in seven; Rams/Raiders drove a large share. Buffalo 72.6% zone, 19.3% man in archived Sharp snapshot, no receiver-specific charting.
- **Buffalo pass-game redistribution:** Week 8 targets were DJ Moore 7, Khalil Shakir 6, Dalton Kincaid 6, Keon Coleman 3. Joshua Palmer ruled OUT. Moore and Kincaid offer different downfield/intermediate roles; Shakir a shorter-area role. Do not project all four at their ceiling simultaneously.
- **Minnesota pass-game bottleneck:** Carson Wentz starts instead of Kyler Murray (out). His two 2026 appearances total 23/39, 276 passing yards, six sacks. 2026 target connection in those appearances: Jefferson 8/12 for 119; Hockenson 6/8 for 58; Addison 2/7 for 21. Small two-appearance sample, not seven full starts.
- **Minnesota running workload shift:** Mason returned from IR in Week 7 (15 rushes), then led 8 to Jones 5 in Week 8; earlier Jones' 110-carry season total reflects Mason's weeks out. Do NOT use Jones' 15.7 seasonal carries/game as his current committee expectation.
- **Availability final gate:** Bills LT Dion Dawkins, LG Alec Anderson questionable; Vikings S Theo Jackson, S Jay Ward questionable. Bills WR Joshua Palmer out; Vikings QB Murray and WR Jauan Jennings out. Bills WR Moore, Coleman and TE Kincaid carry no final game designation. ## Decision hierarchy / contradictions to surface visibly

1. **Most repeatable opportunity:** Cook carries/rushing near 80, Shakir short-area catches. Neither is a verified edge without a price and the full injury confirmation.
2. **Best under-warning roles:** Coleman 2.5 receptions; Addison 3.5; Hockenson 4.5, conditional and with sample-size warnings.
3. **Highest sportsbook-versus-defense tension:** Kincaid 36.5 yards vs Minnesota's TE-group suppression (line already dropped 10), Jefferson 77.5 yards despite Wentz (line rose 8), Allen 30.5 rushing vs unusually high blitz with explicit QB containment awareness.
4. **Explosive-play volatility:** Moore deep targets, Addison deep targets, Jefferson chunk gains, Allen escape runs. Tail-risk belongs in the distribution; season YPG cannot justify a 70–80% over probability on its own.
5. **No strong lean:** Minnesota RB seasonal rushing averages, Moore 47.5 yardage, Kincaid 36.5, Jefferson 77.5 without reliable pass-volume/air-yard scenarios.
6. **Pregame knockout gates:** Final active OL/DBs, route/role changes, sportsbook line drift, which defensive shells/pressures are *actually* used. No charted individual defender assignment or on-field outcome is claimed pregame.
