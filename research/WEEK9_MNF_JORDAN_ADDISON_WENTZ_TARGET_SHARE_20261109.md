## What the board should conclude instead

Wentz's **overall Addison target allocation is about one in five passes**, not the 2026 six-game Addison target average, and not a stable 3.5 targets per game. His true 2025 share (20.1%) and early 2026 Wentz share (17.9%) are quite close despite enormous passing-volume differences.

The 2026-only catches (2 of 7 targets, 28.6%) are too small and too noisy to override the prior 2025 shared catch volume (21 of 30, 70.0%). Across all six appearances Addison caught 23/37 (62.2%) of targets, though the 2025 and 2026 offensive environments differ. **Do not** label over 3.5 receptions as a strong under based solely on 2026.

Current Jauan Jennings absence potentially frees routes, but destination of targets unknown.

**Updated release decision for Addison 3.5 receptions:** CONDITIONAL / PASS until credible team attempts, route participation, Jefferson/Hockenson availability and a timestamped pregame book price are confirmed. At 20–25 team pass attempts, under has a coherent pathway; at 30–35, the over becomes plausible. A hard under lean based on his 2026-only 2/7 connection would be **an avoidable model error**.

**Other warnings:** Buffalo allowed 254.7 net passing yards per game in the seven-game archive; this is opponent-level and does not establish Addison-specific coverage. Target share alone is not target depth, catchable-target rate, yards/catch or defender responsibility. A 2025 Wentz game with an 81-yard Addison catch inflated his yardage ceiling. Do not promote probability confidence from these splits without matched QB/receiver historical role evidence and calibration.
