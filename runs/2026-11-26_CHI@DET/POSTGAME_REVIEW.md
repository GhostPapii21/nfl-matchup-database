## How it was decided

Detroit led 35–32 after Jahmyr Gibbs’ 70-yard touchdown at 8:20 of the 4th (Jake Bates missed the extra point; Christian Mahogany taunting, 15 yards) and Rome Odunze’s 41-yard touchdown plus Caleb Williams’ two-point run at 7:20.

The next four possessions decided it:

1. **DET, 7:20–5:34, interception.** St. Brown 23 yards deep left (first tackler Cam Lewis, inferred; declined illegal contact on Lewis), Jameson Williams 7, Sione Vaki 2. On 3rd-and-1 at the Chicago 34, Goff’s short-left pass intended for Williams was **INTERCEPTED by Dillon Thieneman (T.J. Edwards also named)** at the Chicago 21 and returned 18 yards to the Chicago 39. Thieneman is VERIFIED (INT). Edwards is VERIFIED as named on the INT play (he also has 1 PD in the box).

2. **CHI, 5:25–2:53, turnover on downs.** Seven plays to the Detroit 42. On 4th-and-3, Williams’ deep-right pass to Jahdae Walker was incomplete, **Kerby Joseph (PBU, VERIFIED)**, Malcolm Rodriguez in brackets (pressure). Anthony Lucas taunting, 15 yards, was enforced after the change of possession, so Detroit started at its own 27, not the 42.

3. **DET, 2:41–2:22, turnover on downs — the stop.** Gibbs immediately ran 34 yards left tackle to the Chicago 39 (first tackler Thieneman, inferred). After a Vaki rush and an incompletion to Brock Wright, Goff found LaPorta for 3, then on **4th-and-4 at the Chicago 33** threw short left to Gibbs for only 1 yard (T.J. Edwards, inferred). Chicago took over at its 32 with 2:17 left, trailing 32–35.

4. **CHI, 2:17–0:03, game-winning 13-play, 68-yard drive.** Highlights: Swift 6; **Williams 2-yard right end on 4th-and-1** (McCreary, inferred) — this is Caleb’s third official rush and the carry that moved him to 15 yards; McCreary PBU (VERIFIED) on Carlson; Odunze 11; D.J. Reed face-mask, 15 yards; Ahmed 5; Swift 5 and 12 to the 9; Swift 3 to the 6; Derrick Barnes PBU (VERIFIED) on Swift at 0:13; **Odunze 6-yard touchdown, short right, at 0:03.** The replay official upheld the catch. Cairo Santos is 4-for-4 on extra points; the ensuing kickoff play opens at 39–35, so the PAT was good (not broken out as its own PBP line).

5. **DET, 0:03, end of game.** After a touchback, Goff completed short left to St. Brown for 9 yards, then a series of laterals (Williams, Gibbs, Goff, Gibbs). **Penalty on Gibbs, illegal forward pass**, enforced at the Chicago 39. End of game.

The missed Bates extra point after the 70-yard touchdown was not the decider by itself (if it had been good, Chicago still goes ahead 39–36 with the final PAT). The Thieneman interception and the 4th-and-4 stop at the Chicago 32 were the defensive plays that set up the winning drive.

All receiving legs were cut (no verified main defender). **Right**
- **Caleb U22.5 hit.** Official 15 yards (sim median had been 14). Three scrambles / a 4th-and-1 keep; the 2-point rush was excluded correctly. No designed-run news.
- **Receiving-leg cuts were process-correct.** There was still no VERIFIED main defender on St. Brown’s or Odunze’s big plays (tacklers only, or no one named on the TDs). Cutting those legs was the standing rule, even though St. Brown (146) and Odunze (87, 2 TD) produced.

- **Close final margin / Chicago side of the number.** CHI 39–35 would have cashed a CHI −1.5 spread. That is not the same as getting the scoring environment right.
- **Loveland-out TE handoff.** Once Loveland was ruled out, Carlson and then Roush (5/5, 31) were the tight ends who actually touched the ball.

**Missed, and why (from the data)**
- **“Both run defenses are walls” was wrong.** Gibbs 21–205–3, including 70-, 34-, and 32-yard runs. Swift 30-yard TD, Ahmed 46-yard run. Detroit 246 rush yards, 7.9 per carry. The pregame run-D ranking did not hold in this game.
- **Game total 55.5–56 was buried.** Final combined points: **74**. Both offenses kept hitting explosives after every answer. The 8:58 AM score anchors (CHI 28.5 / DET 27) were far too low.
- **Gibbs vs 92.5.** Pregame packet 82.7 and sim 105 already disagreed with the 92.5 line; he was left as no-play / manual review. The outcome (205) was well above both ranges. The miss is the explosive ceiling, not the carry share (that part we had).

- **LaPorta’s first-half “disappearance” did not last.** 0 targets until 0:06 of the 2nd, then a TD and 4/5 for 44. Live first-half reads should not have been frozen as a full-game fade.
- **Third-down and finishing.** Detroit’s 4-of-12 third downs, the INT, the 4th-and-4 failure, and the missed extra point are why 549 yards and 35 points lost. That closing script was not in the pregame packet.
