## How it was decided

Dallas led 14–0 after one quarter, 28–10 at halftime, and 31–10 after Brandon Aubrey’s 59-yard field goal at 3:35 of the 3rd. Dallas never trailed until the final kick. The Eagles closed a 21-point deficit in the last 15:15 of game clock.

The plays that flipped it:

1. **PHI, 3:35–0:15 3rd, 80 yards, touchdown.** After Aubrey’s 59-yarder made it 10–31, Joe Milton hit DeVonta Smith for 14 (Cobie Durant, inferred), Makai Lemon for 11 (DeMarvion Overshown, inferred; Lemon fumbled out of bounds, not a turnover; Quinnen Williams injured), then **Smith 45 yards deep right for a touchdown at 0:15, no tackler named.** Elliott PAT good, 17–31.

2. **DAL, 0:15 3rd–13:46 4th, blocked punt touchdown.** Javonte Williams 5, Jashaun Corbin 4 (Gabe Hall injured on the play; no return update in the PBP), Williams no gain, delay of game, then on **4th-and-6 from the Dallas 30**, Bryan Anger’s punt was **BLOCKED by Ty Robinson**, recovered by **Kenny Moore II at the Dallas 3 and returned 3 yards for a touchdown.** Elliott PAT good, 24–31. This is Philadelphia’s defensive/special-teams touchdown in the team box.

3. **DAL, 13:46–10:11 4th, answers immediately.** Dak Prescott sacked −6 by Jalen Carter, then **CeeDee Lamb 39 yards deep left** (Jeremiah Trotter Jr., inferred) to the Philadelphia 32, Lamb 12 short middle (Moore, inferred). On 2nd-and-6 at the 14, **Prescott scrambled left end 14 yards for a touchdown.** Aubrey PAT good, 24–38.

4. **PHI, 10:11–9:09 4th, 42 yards in three plays.** Britain Covey returned the kickoff **55 yards to the Dallas 42** (Caleb Downs; Aubrey). Smith 4 short left (Joey Porter Jr., inferred), **Smith 23 yards deep left** (Jalen Thompson, inferred) to the 15, then **Milton scrambled left end 15 yards for a touchdown.** Two-point pass to Zach Ertz incomplete, attempt fails. 30–38.

5. **DAL three-and-out; PHI interception.** After a holding penalty wiped out a Hunt sack, Prescott threw incomplete to KaVontae Turpin, then completed to George Pickens for 4 with an illegal-forward-pass penalty on Pickens. Punt; Covey 23 yards to the Dallas 49 (Dallas challenged down-by-contact; upheld). Milton incomplete deep left to Smith (**Malik Hooker PBU, VERIFIED**; Smith injured on the play), sacked −5 by Overshown, then **intercepted deep right intended for Lemon by Cobie Durant (VERIFIED)** at the Dallas 38, returned 14 to the Philadelphia 48. 30–38, 7:05 left.

6. **DAL, 7:05–3:27, missed field goal.** Six plays to the Philadelphia 22. On 4th-and-5, **Aubrey’s 40-yard field goal, no good, wide left.**

7. **PHI, 3:23–2:10, 70 yards, touchdown.** Injury update: Smith has returned. Darius Cooper 14 (Durant, inferred), Elijah Moore 23 short right (Justin Barron, inferred) and 9 (Porter, inferred), then **Lemon 24 yards deep right for a touchdown, no tackler named.** Two-point pass to Lemon incomplete, attempt fails. A second two-point pass to Smith is complete in the PBP but is **negated by Jordan Mailata offensive holding (no play).** Official score after the try: **36–38.** (The ESPN recap describes a breakup after the holding penalty; that retry is not a separate play in this PBP feed.)

8. **DAL, 2:10–0:55, turnover on downs — the stop.** Williams 2, Spann-Ford 15 to the Philadelphia 48, then three Williams runs to the Philadelphia 39. On **4th-and-1 at the Philadelphia 39, with 0:55 left and Philadelphia out of timeouts**, Prescott was **sacked −9 by Ty Robinson.** Philadelphia took over at its 48.

9. **PHI, 0:50–0:02, game-winning field goal.** Incomplete deep left to Cooper (Thompson injured, later returned); Smith 9 (Downs, inferred); Markquese Bell DPI on Ertz, 2 yards, **VERIFIED**, no play; incomplete short left (no intended receiver named); Smith 5 (Durant, inferred) to the Dallas 36; false start, PBP text PHI-M.Bell (the only Bell in the ESPN box is Dallas S Markquese Bell); incomplete short right to Will Shipley; **Elliott 59-yard field goal good at 0:02.** 39–38.

10. **DAL, 0:02, end of game.** Prescott’s deep-left pass intended for Pickens was **INTERCEPTED by Andrew Mukuba (VERIFIED)** at the Philadelphia 28.
